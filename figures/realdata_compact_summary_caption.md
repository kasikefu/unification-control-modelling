Figure 4: Discovery overlap and GO biological-process enrichment across 213 perturbations. (a,b) Exclusive intersections under non-robust and robust inference. Counts represent significant perturbation–outcome gene pairs; outcomes found for different perturbations are counted separately. Filled dots mark included methods and pale dots mark excluded methods. The IV/SV intersection excluding GMM contains 48 and 17 pairs, respectively. Zero-effect tests use BH q ≤ 0.05 within each perturbation and method over 2,000 outcomes. (c) GMM and batch-stratified Wilcoxon discovery counts. (d) The 12 GO terms with the greatest summed numbers of enriched perturbations across selections. Enrichment uses the tested outcomes as background and BH q ≤ 0.05 within each perturbation and selection.
